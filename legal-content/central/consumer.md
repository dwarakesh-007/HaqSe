# Consumer problems

## What counts as a consumer problem
You are a "consumer" if you bought goods or paid for a service for your own use (not to resell). This includes online shopping.

Common problems:
- Defective or damaged product delivered
- Wrong product delivered
- Refund or replacement refused
- Warranty or guarantee not honoured
- Charged more than the MRP or hidden charges
- Poor or incomplete service (courier, telecom, bank, insurance, coaching, builders, hospitals, airlines)
- Misleading advertisements
- Unfair terms in a contract

## Your rights (simple words)
- Right to safety: products should not harm you.
- Right to information: you must be told the price, quality, quantity and expiry.
- Right to choose.
- Right to be heard: your complaint must be listened to.
- Right to get a remedy: refund, replacement, repair, or compensation.

## Step-by-step path
1. **Complain to the seller or company first.** Email or write to their customer care. Keep a copy. Give them a clear deadline, usually 15 days.
2. **If they ignore you, go to the National Consumer Helpline (NCH).**
   - Call 1915, or file online at consumerhelpline.gov.in.
   - NCH contacts the company for you. Many cases are settled here for free.
3. **If still not solved, file a complaint at the Consumer Commission.**
   - You can file online through e-Daakhil (edaakhil.nic.in).
   - You do not need a lawyer. You can argue your own case.
   - You can file where you live or work, not only where the company is.

## Which commission (based on the value of goods or services paid)
- District Commission: up to Rs 50 lakh
- State Commission: Rs 50 lakh to Rs 2 crore
- National Commission: above Rs 2 crore

(Limits from the 2021 rules. Check before launch.)

## Time limit
File within **2 years** from the date the problem happened.

## Fees
No fee for complaints up to Rs 5 lakh. Small fees above that. Check the current fee table on e-Daakhil.

## Evidence to keep
- Bill, invoice or order confirmation
- Payment proof (UPI screenshot, bank statement)
- Photos or videos of the defect
- All emails, chats and call records with the company
- Warranty card

## When this app should say "get human help"
- Medical negligence cases
- Builder or real-estate cases with large amounts
- When the company has filed a case against the user
Point the user to the District Legal Services Authority (DLSA) for free legal aid.
